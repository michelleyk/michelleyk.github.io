About us

Welcome to batvision We may be a bit “bat” biased… Here, we view everything from our own unique vision, and we hope you do too. We invite you to explore your vision in this world, dive in!

batvision is your neighborhood comic cave for new releases, legendary back issues, graphic novels, and the kind of deep-cut discoveries that help you see further than you ever have before. Whether you’re a lifelong collector, a casual reader, or stepping into comics for the very first time, we’re here to help you find stories that click with your tastes.

Check out our menu for insights into the latest releases, our newest in store, and even curated bundles—perfect for novice collectors or as gifts.

Come by for new comic day excitement, staff picks, themed displays, reading recommendations, and events—signings, clubs, release parties, creator spotlights, and more as our roster grows. If you want consistency, start a pull list and we’ll keep your favorites set aside, ready for pickup as a weekly ritual.

Your vision belongs here.
